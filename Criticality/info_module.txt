USER GUIDE

This window is the approach-to-criticality calculator. It finds the boric acid
concentration change and the make-up pump time needed to reach criticality.
What is computed and the formulas behind it are in the General manual.

There are three tabs:
    • Forecast   - the plain material-balance calculation
    • Insertion  - insertion of the top three groups in the regular sequence
    • Extraction - extraction of the top three groups, one block each


1. Working with the tables

Each tab is an editable table:
    • Green cells are inputs - double-click to edit them
    • Yellow cells are the results
    • White cells are intermediate values

Editing a green input recomputes every result at once. Move around with the
arrow keys; Enter opens the editor on the focused cell. Both "." and "," work
as the decimal separator.


2. The formula panel

The key formulas are shown in a small panel in the lower-right corner, over
the table. Double-click the panel to hide it; it can be shown or hidden again
from "View -> Show / hide formulas".


3. Group mode

"Mode settings -> Switch 12 <-> 10 groups" switches the mode. The three moving
groups are numbered 10, 11, 12 in the 12-group mode and 8, 9, 10 in the
10-group mode; the calculation is the same three groups.


4. Menu and buttons

    • Save - "Export all pages to Excel" writes all three tabs into one .xlsx
      file, one sheet per tab, keeping the green/yellow coloring.
    • Mode settings - the group mode, see section 3.
    • View - show or hide the formula panel.
    • INFO - this guide.

At the bottom: "<< BACK" returns to the main menu, "Plots >>" opens the
plotting window, and "Export all to Excel" exports the workbook.


5. Notes

This experiment needs beta (set in the main window) but no loaded file. The
inputs and results are held only while the window is open; export to Excel to
keep them.
