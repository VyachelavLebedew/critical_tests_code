USER GUIDE

This window determines the power reactivity coefficient - PCR. The coefficient
is measured in two procedures (with and without steam extraction) and the two
results are combined. Here you mark the record and read the result; the
physics and the formulas are in the General manual.


1. What is on screen

On the left is the plot: reactivity (blue), temperature (red) and pressure
(green). On the right is the results table, with one column per procedure
(with steam, without steam) plus the weighted mean. The splitter between them
adjusts their size.

The current steam mode is shown above the plot. Two status lines below it show
a clock for a procedure not yet computed and a tick once it is.


2. Marking a procedure

The clicks depend on the steam mode; a hint in the corner shows which click
is expected next.

Without steam extraction:
    1. Click the steady section before the reactivity is added (the base);
       it is marked with a solid black line.
    2. Press "Get DTDt RCPS" to fix the base.
    3. Click the start of the power rise (t0), then its end (t1); both are
       marked with dashed brown lines.

With steam extraction: there is no base, so "Get DTDt RCPS" is disabled and
only t0 and t1 are clicked.

Re-clicking. Once all points are placed, a further click moves the nearest
line, both before and after "Proceed", so any point can be corrected.

If, at t1, the net heating rate is not above the expected 10 degC/h, or (without
steam) the group stood at the same position for the base and for t0/t1, a
confirmation is asked before the result is accepted.


3. Computing and the second procedure

Press "Proceed" to compute the current procedure; its column fills in. The
deviation from the reference is shown only if a reference PCR was entered.

Use "Options -> Switch mode" to switch to the other steam mode - the first
result stays in the table - mark the second procedure and press "Proceed"
again. The "Mean" column then shows the weighted mean of the two.

If the two procedures are in separate files, use "Options -> Load a new file".
The columns are re-resolved by their header names. If a header is missing, the
file is not loaded and the missing columns are listed.


4. The results table

Each procedure column lists R(t0)/R(t1), T(t0)/T(t1)/ΔT, P(t0)/P(t1)/ΔP,
v_T(t1), v_RCPS, N(t1), ΔR_N, PCR_exp, its uncertainty, the reference and the
deviation. The "Mean" column shows the weighted PCR_exp and its deviation.

Reactivity is shown in % by default. Use the "Units" menu to switch R(t0),
R(t1) and ΔR_N between % and Beff.


5. Menu

    • Units - the reactivity unit (% or Beff).
    • Decimal - the number of decimal places for reactivity, temperature,
      pressure, and general (other values).
    • Plot - "Plot in new window" opens a synchronized enlarged copy;
      "Extended cursor" toggles a crosshair with the current values; "Show /
      hide legend" toggles the legend.
    • Save - the results as .txt (tab-separated) or .xlsx.


6. Navigation

"BACK" returns to the parameter window. The computed results are not preserved
after leaving this window.
